About

            

              Blockcraft is an original voxel sandbox, heavily inspired by
              Minecraft by Mojang Studios. It is not affiliated with,
              endorsed by, or connected to Mojang or Microsoft in any way, and
              shares no code or artwork with it. Everything here — the
              terrain generation, the renderer, the physics, and every built-in
              texture — was written from scratch.
            


            
Resource packs

            

              Textures you load are the work of their authors and stay on your
              machine — the game reads them at runtime and never
              redistributes them. Please respect each pack's licence.
