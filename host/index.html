Letting friends in

Your computer sits behind a router, so a server running here is not reachable from outside your own network until you do one of these. Both are free.

Forward the port

Tell your router to send traffic on your port to this computer. No third party involved, and nothing to keep running — but you need access to the router, and some connections cannot do it at all.

Use a tunnel

A tunnel gives you a public address without touching the router. Free tiers exist that are enough for a small server. Paste the address it gives you below and the app will share that instead.

Name

A .bc name is what people actually remember. It resolves inside Blockcraft only — it is not a real domain, so it will not ping and will not open in a browser.
